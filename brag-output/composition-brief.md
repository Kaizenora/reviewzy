## Storyboard
1. **Scene 1: The Counter Stand (0.0s - 3.5s)**
   - Reveal the luxury counter stand with Google "Review us on Google" badge & 5 stars.
   - Text overlay: "Turn counter visits into Google reviews in 30 seconds."
2. **Scene 2: Instant Scan & Tag Selection (3.5s - 8.0s)**
   - Smooth zoom into the phone UI.
   - User taps: `Tasty food` ✓, `Friendly staff` ✓, `Nice ambience` ✓.
   - Vibrant orange glows with soft audio pops.
3. **Scene 3: AI Auto-Drafting (8.0s - 13.0s)**
   - Typewriter text animates into view with realistic typing rhythm.
   - Tone pills highlight: `Rewrite`, `Shorter`, `Hinglish`.
   - Text: "Zero typing fatigue. 100% authentic praise."
4. **Scene 4: The 1-Tap Google Launch (13.0s - 16.5s)**
   - Big CTA button: `Copy review & open Google ↗`.
   - Tap action triggers multi-colored confetti particles!
   - Instant deep-link into native Google Maps with review pasted.
5. **Scene 5: Outro & Slate (16.5s - 19.0s)**
   - Reviewzy logotype + "More 5-Star Reviews. Zero Friction."
   - URL: `reviewzy.vercel.app`
   - Bottom badge: "Designed by SG" with the gold monogram.
