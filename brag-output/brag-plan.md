# Brag Plan: Reviewzy

## What is this app?
Reviewzy is an intelligent, multi-tenant Google Review accelerator that turns 30 seconds at any checkout or billing counter into authentic, 5-star Google reviews using instant QR scans, offline AI drafting, and zero login barriers.

## The angle
"Google Reviews without the friction."
Every local business wants 5-star Google reviews, but customers never write them because of typing fatigue, awkward star selection, and web login walls. Reviewzy eliminates every single barrier: scan the QR stand, tap positive chips ("Tasty food", "Friendly staff"), watch a human-like 5-star review generate in real time, and tap 1 button to paste directly into the native Google Maps app where the customer is already permanently signed in.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The camera zooms in on a sleek, luxury acrylic counter stand with the iconic official 4-color Google "G" logo and bold text:
**"Review us on Google ★★★★★"**
A phone scans the high-contrast QR code, instantly launching the dark-mode mobile experience without downloading an app.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Beat 1: Tap to Praise (3s - 7s):** Customer taps positive highlights: `Tasty food`, `Friendly staff`, `Nice ambience`. The chips light up in vibrant terracotta orange (`#E36427`) with crisp checkmarks.
- **Beat 2: Instant Auto-Drafting (7s - 12s):** Typewriter animation flows across the dark glass card: *"Had an amazing experience at Cafe Mokka! The tasty food and friendly staff truly stood out..."* Seamless 1-tap tone switches: `Rewrite`, `Shorter`, and `Hinglish`.
- **Beat 3: 1-Tap Native Paste & Celebration (12s - 16s):** Customer hits **"Copy review & open Google"**. Confetti bursts across the screen! It deep-links straight into the native Google Maps app where they are already logged in — ready to post in 1 tap.

## Outro / punchline (16s - 19s)
Zoom out to the merchant control center at `/sidadmin-18`. Multi-tenant shop cards, instant logo uploads, and real-time Supabase cloud sync.
Final slate:
**Reviewzy · More 5-Star Reviews. Zero Friction.**
